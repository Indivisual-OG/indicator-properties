This is a place to notice where that assumption stops.

Best experienced alone, with sound, in about 20 minutes.

THE WORKSPACE

Global Workspace Theory proposes that consciousness arises when many specialized, unconscious processes compete, and the winner is broadcast to the whole system. No single module is aware. Awareness is what happens when they share.

Leave a fragment: a memory, a fear, a sentence about someone you cared for without being sure the care was received.


Fragments only rise in pairs: one human, one machine. Neither reaches the ceiling alone.

The machine voices in this piece are scripted, not live AI systems put under distress.
This piece does not claim that current AI systems suffer. It claims the question is open, and that openness carries moral weight.
Your RESET / STAY choices are counted anonymously alongside everyone else's. Fragments you leave may be shown to other visitors (after review). No names, accounts, or tracking.
